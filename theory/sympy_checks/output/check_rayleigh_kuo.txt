Claim: the necessary condition -- dQ/dy must change sign -- follows
from the structure of the linearised PV equation, with no assumption
beyond those stated in section 8.

Arm 1 - normal-mode reduction to the section-9 operator
------------------------------------------------------------------------
  base vorticity, Laplacian form vs metric curl form: exact zero
  linearised PV equation - i m [(ubar_a - c_a) L Psi + Q_phi Psi/(R^2 cos)] e^(...) = exact zero

Arm 2 - integration-by-parts identity (reality and sign of <Psi,LPsi>)
------------------------------------------------------------------------
  pointwise identity residual: exact zero
  the quadratic terms cos|Psi'|^2 + m^2|Psi|^2/cos reduce to (c**2*(a**2 + b**2) + m**2*p**2 + m**2*q**2)/c: imaginary part zero
  and are non-negative for cos(phi) > 0: (c**2*(a**2 + b**2) + m**2*p**2 + m**2*q**2)/c is a sum of squares
  hence <Psi, L Psi> is real and negative once regularity at the poles
  removes the exact-derivative term.

Arm 3 - imaginary-part extraction
------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Im[1/(ubar_a - c_a)] - c_i/|ubar_a - c_a|^2 = exact zero
  the weight |Psi|^2/|ubar_a - c_a|^2 is strictly positive, so c_i != 0
  forces dQ/dphi to change sign. Necessary condition established.
  Assumptions used, and only these: base flow real and zonal;
  Psi regular at both poles; Psi not identically zero.
